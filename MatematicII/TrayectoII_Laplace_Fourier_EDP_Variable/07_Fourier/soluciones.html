📝 Soluciones - Ejercicios

1. \(f(x) \sim \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{2(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}\sin(n\pi x)\)
2. \(f(x) \sim \frac{4}{3} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{16(-1)^n}{n^2\pi^2}\cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}\right)\)
3. \(f(x) \sim \frac{1}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{2}{(2n-1)\pi}\sin((2n-1)x)\)
4. \(f(x) \sim \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{4}{\pi}\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\cos((2n-1)x)}{(2n-1)^2}\)
5. \(f(x) \sim \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{2(1-(-1)^n)}{n\pi}\sin(nx)\)
6. Converge a \(0\) (promedio de los límites laterales).
7. Aproximadamente el 9% del salto.
8. \(f(x) \sim \frac{4A}{\pi}\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{2n-1}\sin\left(\frac{(2n-1)\pi x}{L}\right)\)
9. \(f(x) \sim \frac{L}{2} - \frac{4L}{\pi^2}\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\cos((2n-1)\pi x/L)}{(2n-1)^2}\)
